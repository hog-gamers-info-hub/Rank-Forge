Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to PointIQ, published on Google Play under the developer name Sunil Kumar | PointIQ. This Privacy Policy explains how PointIQ collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the PointIQ Android application and related services.

1. Account Information

PointIQ allows users to create and access an account using email and password authentication or Google Sign-In.

Depending on the authentication method you use, PointIQ may process:

  • Your email address
  • A unique user account identifier
  • Basic profile information, such as your name, if it is provided through Google Sign-In

This information is used for account creation, authentication, account management, secure access to your data, synchronization, and restoration of your PointIQ information.

2. Photos and Screenshots

PointIQ allows you to select images from your device for features such as tournament processing, OCR-based result processing, lobby processing, match result processing, and custom designs.

Selected images may include:

  • Match lobby screenshots
  • Match result screenshots
  • Images used for custom tournament result designs

Depending on the feature being used, images may be processed on your device and/or uploaded to PointIQ's cloud storage so that the application can provide its functionality, synchronize data, restore information, and maintain user-selected designs or screenshots.

PointIQ does not collect videos as part of its normal application functionality.

3. Tournament and User-Generated Content

When you create and manage tournaments, PointIQ may store information you provide or generate through the application, including:

  • Tournament information
  • Team names and team slot information
  • Player names
  • Match information
  • Placements
  • Kill information
  • Points and scoring information
  • Tournament standings and results
  • Screenshot-related metadata
  • Custom design settings

This information may be stored locally on your device and may also be synchronized with PointIQ's cloud services when required for application functionality.

4. Sharing Tournament Standings

PointIQ includes a feature that allows you to intentionally publish tournament standings and create a shareable link.

When you choose to use this feature, the published standings may include information such as:

  • Team names
  • Team rankings
  • Total points
  • Position points
  • Kill points
  • First-place finishes
  • Match-related standings information

Sharing is initiated by you. Anyone who receives or obtains the generated sharing link may be able to view the published standings.

You should avoid including personal or sensitive information in tournament names, team names, player names, or other content that you do not want to appear in shared tournament information.

5. How We Use Information

Information processed by PointIQ may be used to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts
  • Provide tournament creation and management features
  • Process screenshots and OCR results
  • Calculate match and tournament scores
  • Store and restore tournament information
  • Synchronize information between your device and cloud services
  • Store user-selected screenshots and custom design assets
  • Generate tournament results and standings
  • Provide user-initiated standings sharing
  • Protect user accounts and application data
  • Process account and data deletion requests

PointIQ does not use your personal information for advertising or targeted advertising.

6. Service Providers

PointIQ uses third-party service providers where necessary to operate the application.

Supabase

Supabase is used for services including user authentication, database storage, cloud synchronization, backend functionality, and storage of certain images and application data.

Google

If you choose Google Sign-In, Google provides authentication services and may provide basic account information required to complete authentication.

These service providers process information as necessary to provide their respective services.

PointIQ does not sell your personal information.

7. Information PointIQ Does Not Collect for Its Own Purposes

PointIQ does not intentionally collect the following categories of information for its own application purposes:

  • Precise or approximate location
  • SMS, email, or other private message contents
  • Web browsing history
  • Lists of installed applications
  • Health information
  • Financial information
  • Advertising ID for advertising purposes
  • Video files

PointIQ does not contain advertisements and does not use user information for advertising or targeted marketing.

8. Data Security

PointIQ takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information.

These measures include:

  • Encrypted network connections when communicating with cloud services
  • Authenticated access to user accounts
  • Account-scoped access controls for cloud data
  • Application-private local storage where appropriate

However, no electronic storage system or method of transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Cloud-stored account and application data may be retained while your PointIQ account remains active and while the information is required to provide application functionality.

When you permanently delete your PointIQ account using the provided account deletion process, PointIQ will delete the account and associated PointIQ cloud data according to the application's account deletion process.

Certain information may be retained where required by applicable law or where necessary for legitimate legal obligations.

Information that you separately export, download, copy, or intentionally share outside PointIQ-controlled storage may remain outside PointIQ's control.

10. Account and Data Deletion

PointIQ provides users with the ability to permanently delete their account and associated PointIQ cloud data.

You can request account deletion using the account deletion feature available within the PointIQ application.

You can also access the external account deletion page at:

https://hog-gamers-info-hub.github.io/Rank-Forge/delete-account/

Authentication may be required before an account can be deleted to verify that the deletion request is being made by the account owner.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone once completed.

11. Local Device Data

PointIQ may store application data locally on your device, including data required for offline functionality, restoration, application state, and locally stored tournament information.

Uninstalling PointIQ may remove local application data from your device, but uninstalling the application does not automatically delete your PointIQ cloud account or cloud-stored information.

To permanently delete your PointIQ account and associated cloud data, use the account deletion process described above.

12. Third-Party Links and External Applications

PointIQ may provide links or actions that open external applications or services, including Google, Gmail, Instagram, and WhatsApp.

When you leave PointIQ and use a third-party application or service, that service's own privacy policy and terms apply. PointIQ is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

13. Children's Privacy

PointIQ is not directed to children. PointIQ does not knowingly design its services to collect personal information from children for advertising, profiling, or similar purposes.

If PointIQ becomes aware that personal information has been collected in circumstances where applicable law requires its deletion, appropriate steps will be taken to delete that information.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PointIQ's features, data practices, legal requirements, or third-party services change.

When this Privacy Policy is updated, the effective date shown at the top of this page will also be updated.
